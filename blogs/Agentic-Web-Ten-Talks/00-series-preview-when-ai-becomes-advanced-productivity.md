# Preview: *Agentic Web — Ten Talks*

## When AI Becomes an Advanced Productive Force, Why Must the Internet Be Rebuilt?

At the end of 2025, an unassuming event triggered intense discussion across the internet industry: Doubao's phone assistant, developed by ByteDance, was restricted and even banned by platforms such as WeChat because it could complete tasks across applications.

At first glance, this looked like yet another competitive conflict between platforms. But we believe its essence goes far beyond that.

**This was the first time an advanced productive force had collided head-on with the old Internet's relations of production.**

---

## A Fundamental Question That Has Been Overlooked

Over the past thirty years, we have watched the Internet move from openness toward enclosure:

- When Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web, his original vision was that “connection is power”—that anyone should be free to connect information.
- The HTTP protocol succeeded because it was simple, open, and permissionless, laying the foundation for the flourishing of Web 1.0.
- Yet today, super-platforms dominate everything, data silos stand everywhere, and openness seems like a thing of the past.

**Why did a network born to enable “connection” ultimately become closed?**

Many people attribute this to commercial greed or moral decline, but that explanation is too simple. We believe enclosure was not a conspiracy; under the technological conditions of the time, it was the Internet's optimal solution.

**The arrival of AI is changing that premise at its root.** The biggest change is that the subjects of the Internet are shifting from people and software to intelligent agents.

This will push the Internet into a new phase: **a return to openness.**

That is the subject we want to explore: the Agentic Web, an Internet shaped by intelligent agents. I believe this is the true next-generation Web—Web 3.0—an Internet whose defining characteristic is openness.

## Why Launch a Series on the Agentic Web?

Over the past year, while building the ANP open-source community, I have had the opportunity to exchange ideas with many people in the industry. Through those conversations, a framework for thinking about the Agentic Web gradually took shape and continued to evolve. At this point, it feels necessary to organize these ideas systematically. Writing public articles has always been one of the ways I think deeply.

I may use AI as an assistant, but the framework and ideas are entirely my own. Even with AI's help, however, I cannot guarantee a daily release.

My first New Year's resolution: I hope to finish the series.

## What Will *Agentic Web — Ten Talks* Discuss?

Stepping back, in this series I hope to explore the past, present, and future of the Internet of intelligent agents in a systematic way.

### Planned Outline

**Day 1: From the World Wide Web to Super-Platforms — The Historical Inevitability of Web Enclosure**
> Why was a network born to enable “connection” bound to become platformized and closed?

**Day 2: Why Does AI Need an Open Internet?**
> What are the first principles behind the inevitable return of the Internet to openness?

**Day 3: The First Head-On Collision — Doubao's Phone Assistant and the Old Internet's Relations of Production**
> Doubao is not merely a product incident; it is the first direct collision between an advanced productive force and the old Internet's relations of production.

**Day 4: What Is the Agentic Web? When Intelligent Agents Become the Internet's First Citizens**
> What will the Internet become if intelligent agents—not people clicking web pages—are the ones getting things done?

**Day 5: Three Major Trends Shaping the Agentic Web**
> Is the Agentic Web a vision of the future, or is it already happening?

**Day 6: Agent Protocols — The HTTP of the AI Era Is Taking Shape**
> User interfaces are designed for people; protocols are designed for AI.

**Day 7: When Agents Start Completing Tasks on Their Own — How Applications and Workflows Will Be Rebuilt**
> What happens to the Internet when the executor changes from a person to an intelligent agent?

**Day 8: From the Human Internet to the Agent Internet — How the Architecture Is Evolving**
> What are the three core trends in the evolution of the Internet's architecture?

**Day 9: How Will the Agentic Web Make Money Without Platform Commissions?**
> After de-platformization, where will the money come from? Will new platforms emerge?

**Day 10: Who Is Defining the Internet of the Future? The Agentic Web's Path and Landscape**
> As the Agentic Web takes shape, who are its major players, and how will they compete?
